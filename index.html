Jarly

Personal finance
Coming soon

Overview

A budgeting app that gives you a method, not just a ledger. Pick a finance model — Six Jars, 50/30/20 or Pay Yourself First — and Jarly splits your income into its buckets, charging every expense to the right one.

The question it answers is not "what did I spend?" but "what is left in each bucket?" Fully offline: no account, no server, no ads.
