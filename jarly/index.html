Jarly splits your income into the buckets of a proven budget model and charges every expense to the right one. So instead of asking what you spent, you see what you can still spend.

Set up once, then just log what you spend

Setup takes about two minutes and nothing is saved until you finish it.

  1. Pick a model

    Answer two questions and Jarly suggests a model, with one sentence on why. The other two are right next to it.

  2. Make the split yours

    Move the sliders until the percentages fit your life. If they don't add up to 100, Jarly tells you by how much.

  3. Spend from a jar

    Every expense has a category, and every category belongs to a jar. Log a coffee and the right jar goes down.
