    Three answers, one date

    Setup takes a few minutes. Copy the numbers from your statements and Zeroly does the math.

    1. Add what you owe

      Cards, loans, money you borrowed from someone. Enter the balance, the interest rate and the minimum payment as they appear on your statement.

    2. Say what you can pay

      One monthly amount for all your debts together. If it doesn't cover the minimums, Zeroly tells you exactly how much is missing.

    3. Pay what the list says

      Each month you get a short list of what to pay and where. Log a payment and your date updates.

    Everything points to one date

    Two proven methods. Zeroly picks with you.

    Both pay every minimum. They differ in where the extra money goes.

    Snowball

    Smallest balance first

    You close a debt early, and its payment rolls into the next one. Quick wins keep you going.

    Avalanche

    Highest interest first

    The most expensive debt goes first, so you pay the least interest overall. It can take longer to see the first one close.

    During setup Zeroly asks what keeps you going, compares both methods on your real debts, and explains its pick in one sentence. You can switch at any time.
